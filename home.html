On-the-Job Training (OJT)

Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) R10 — Manolo Fortich Fire Station · Northern Bukidnon State College

Completed my OJT with the Bureau of Fire Protection at the Manolo Fortich Fire Station in partnership with Northern Bukidnon State College. The training focused on applying IT skills in a public-service setting—supporting fire-prevention outreach, station operations, and collaborative projects such as community fire-safety programs and digital tools for the station.

Highlights included hands-on work with BFP personnel, participation in culminating activities with fellow NBSC students, and contributing to initiatives that connect technology with community fire safety (“Sa pag-iwas sa sunog, hindi ka nag-iisa”).
